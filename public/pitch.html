What we chose not to fake

The menu OCR is on-device, and it is imperfect.

  • It reads prices reliably and hand-set Chinese names poorly
  • So it never invents a price it could not read — it asks you
  • Every line is editable, and the whole feature degrades to a known menu
  • HKGAI has no vision model today. We did not pretend otherwise.
