# 10-minute recorded pitch — cue card

**8 min presentation + 2 min demo.** Keep this open on your phone beside you.
Everything you need to say is already on the slides in type large enough to
read, so you are prompting from the screen, not reading a script.

**Open the deck by double-clicking `submission/pitch/pitch.html`.** It is
standalone — relative paths, no server — because a dev server that dies
mid-recording costs you the whole take. (`http://localhost:3001/pitch.html`
is the same deck if the server happens to be up, but do not rely on it.)

Press **F** for fullscreen. → or space to advance · ← back.

Record with **⌘⇧5 → Record Entire Screen**, microphone on. **One take.** If you
fumble a sentence, keep going — it is a pitch, not a film.

## If you overrun

You will know by slide 5. If you are more than 20 seconds late there, cut
**slide 3** to a single sentence and **slide 8** to naming the three hubs.
Never rush slides 7 or 9 — 7 is the strongest idea you have, and 9 is the one
that makes a judge trust the rest.

## If you underrun

Do not pad. Ending at 9:30 is fine; a recorded pitch is not marked on hitting
the ceiling.

---

## Before you press record

- Fullscreen the deck (**F**) so no browser chrome is in frame
- Do Not Disturb on, other apps quit
- Play two seconds of the demo once, then press **Home** to return to slide 1 —
  it loads the video so there is no buffering pause mid-take
- Glass of water. You are talking for ten minutes.
