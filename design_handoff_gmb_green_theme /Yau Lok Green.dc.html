Turn 6 — 影餐牌 · Shoot the menu, order off it

影張餐牌落單

A 茶餐廳 menu is a wall of hand-set Chinese with no pictures and no English — the one artefact a visitor cannot get past. The feature turns the photo itself into the interface: shoot the 餐牌, and the app reads it back as tappable rows in place, so you point at what you saw on the wall rather than searching a database that doesn't have this shop in it. Ordering then splits in two — 揀嘢 (what) and 特別要求 (how) — and the "how" is where the shorthand lives: 走冰, 少甜, 茶走, 飛沙走奶, 加底, 走青, 行街. Each modifier chip shows the phrase the kitchen actually reads, so the chit that comes out is a real 落單紙 in a 伙記's own hand, not a translated sentence. Codes follow the same 術語 reference as Turn 4.
