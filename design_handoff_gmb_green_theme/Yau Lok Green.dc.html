Turn 1 — 綠色小巴主題 · Green minibus re-theme

有落 — GMB livery re-theme

Two directions on the same eight screens, differing on how literally the app wears the minibus. Green is the repo's own --sign-green #0f7a52 — already defined as “green minibus, and on the way” — promoted from accent to primary. 1a is the full livery — jade roof, cream body, LED boards doing real work. 1b keeps the LED as a logo and one announcement strip, and lets the rest of the app breathe. Screens are live: ETA counts down, the 有落 button fires, favourites toggle.
